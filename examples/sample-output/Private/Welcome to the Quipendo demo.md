# Welcome to the Quipendo demo

This is a **made-up Quip account**. Every document, person, company and number in it is invented, so you can see what Quipendo makes of a real account without anyone's real documents being involved.

## What is in here

- [Website Relaunch: Project Plan](Projects/Website%20Relaunch/Website%20Relaunch%20-%20Project%20Plan.md): a status bar, calendar, Kanban board and project tracker, images, an attachment, and comments on highlighted text
- [Launch Checklist](Projects/Website%20Relaunch/Launch%20Checklist.md): nested checklists, a countdown, numbered steps and dates
- [Kickoff Deck](Projects/Website%20Relaunch/Kickoff%20Deck.md): a slide deck, saved as a note with a PDF beside it
- [Budget 2026](Projects/Budget%202026.md): a spreadsheet, with its .xlsx saved alongside so the formulas survive
- [Lisbon Weekend](Travel/Lisbon%20Weekend.md): a video, a calendar, an image with a caption, an embedded spreadsheet and a packing list
- [Weekly Meal Planner](Home/Weekly%20Meal%20Planner.md) and [Sourdough Notes](Home/Sourdough%20Notes.md): tables, every kind of text formatting, code and quotes
- [Weekly Sync](../Team%20Shared/Meeting%20Notes/Weekly%20Sync%20-%204%20March%202026.md) and [Retro: Sprint 12](../Team%20Shared/Meeting%20Notes/Retro%20-%20Sprint%2012.md) (in Team Shared): a poll, comments with mentions and replies, a second Kanban board
- [Team Handbook](../Team%20Shared/Team%20Handbook.md) (in Team Shared): a process bar, Salesforce records, a welcome video and a link to a document outside the export

## What to look for

1. The notes sit in the same folders as in Quip: **Private**, **Team Shared** and **Archive**.
2. This note is also on the **Desktop**, so its header says `also_in` and the index lists it once.
3. Links between documents work offline. The one link to a document outside the export is listed in the report.

> Quipendo only ever reads from Quip. It cannot create, change or delete a document.
