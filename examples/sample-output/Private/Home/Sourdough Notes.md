# Sourdough Notes

Our house loaf: **78% hydration**, *slow* and <u>cold</u>, ~~no commercial yeast~~ only wild yeast. The starter is fed 1:5:5 and lives on the second shelf.

## Ingredients

- 500 g strong white flour
- 390 g water
- 10 g fine salt
- 100 g active starter

## Method

1. Mix everything and rest for **30 minutes**.
2. Fold four times, 30 minutes apart.
3. Shape, then proof in the fridge overnight.
4. Bake in a hot dutch oven: 20 minutes lid on, 25 minutes lid off.

### Notes

Dough at 24 °C doubles in about five hours. The hydration is water over flour, so 390 / 500 = 0.78, and in my head a loaf rises as *x*<sup>2</sup>. Water is H<sub>2</sub>O, whatever the loaf thinks.

> Patience is the main ingredient. Everything else is flour.
